# Commercial licensing

Baige (百格附着力分析) is released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**
(see `LICENSE`). AGPL-3.0 covers free use, including internal use inside a company, and
requires that anyone who uses a modified version **over a network** is offered its source.

A **separate commercial licence** is available for cases where AGPL-3.0 does not fit:

| Scenario | AGPL-3.0 (free) | Commercial licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run it for yourself / inside your own company (no distribution) | yes | not needed |
| Modify it and keep the modifications to yourself, no network service | yes | not needed |
| Host it as a network service (SaaS, internal platform with users outside your legal entity) | yes, **but modifications must be published under AGPL-3.0** | yes — modifications may stay closed |
| Bundle it into a closed-source product or an appliance you ship to customers | no (the combined work must be AGPL) | yes |
| Rebrand / white-label it for your customers | no (see the trademark note in `README.md`) | yes |
| Need an indemnity, a warranty, or an SLA | no (AGPL offers none) | negotiable |

## How to ask

Open an issue in this repository with a title starting with **`[commercial]`** and say:

- who you are (company / individual),
- what you want to do (host it, ship it, rebrand it, modify it privately),
- roughly how many users or installations.

Do not put confidential details in the issue — that message is only the first contact;
the rest goes over email.

## Notes

- The AGPL-3.0 grant is irrevocable for every version already published. A commercial
  licence adds an alternative, it does not remove anyone's AGPL rights.
- Contributions are accepted under the Developer Certificate of Origin (`git commit -s`).
  If a contribution is needed for a commercial release, we may ask for a short
  contributor licence agreement — this is what keeps dual licensing possible.
- "Baige" / "百格分析" and the project logo are not covered by either licence
  (AGPL-3.0 §7 and the trademark note in `README.md`).
